Moon Lander: a free Lunar Lander-style game

Steer a lunar module down to the surface. Turn, fire the engine and touch down gently on one of the pads; the narrow ones pay the most.

Play Moon Lander now How to play

How to play Moon Lander

Your lander drifts down toward a jagged moon surface. Gravity pulls it down all the time. Turn it left or right and fire the engine to slow down or move sideways. The engine always pushes in the direction the lander points.

Land on one of the flat pads. You need to be upright and slow: the speed readouts on screen turn green when your horizontal and vertical speed are safe. Touch down anywhere else, or too fast, and the lander crashes.

Every safe landing earns points times the pad's multiplier, plus 400 fuel for the next approach. When the fuel runs out, the engine stops, so plan your descent.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Kill your sideways speed first, high above the ground. Correcting it close to the surface usually means landing tilted.
  2. Use short engine bursts. A long burn wastes fuel and sends you back up.
  3. The narrow pad is worth five times, but a safe landing on a wide pad beats a crash on the narrow one.
