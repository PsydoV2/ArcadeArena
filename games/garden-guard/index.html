Garden Guard: a free Centipede-style shooter

A caterpillar winds its way down through a mushroom garden. Every hit splits it in two and leaves a new mushroom behind, a spider zigzags through your corner and fleas plant more mushrooms.

Play Garden Guard now How to play

How to play Garden Guard

You guard the bottom of a garden with a small blaster that can move left, right and a little way up. A long caterpillar enters at the top and winds its way down, turning around every time it bumps into a mushroom or the edge of the screen.

Every segment you hit turns into a mushroom, and the caterpillar splits into two, each with its own head. Shoot the head for the most points. When the last segment is gone, a new caterpillar arrives in new colors.

A spider zigzags through your area and is worth more the closer it is when you hit it. Fleas drop straight down and plant new mushrooms on the way. Mushrooms take a few shots to clear.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Keep the bottom of the screen clear of mushrooms. Once the caterpillar reaches your area, it has fewer obstacles to turn on and comes at you quickly.
  2. Shoot the head when it comes down a column. A stream of shots up one column can take the whole caterpillar apart segment by segment.
  3. Let the spider come close before you shoot: a point-blank hit is worth 900.
