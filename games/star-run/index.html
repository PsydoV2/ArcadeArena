Star Run: a free 3D cockpit space shooter

Sit in the cockpit and fly through space in 3D. Shoot fighters and asteroids, dodge laser fences and keep your shield up from sector to sector.

Play Star Run now How to play

How to play Star Run

You look out of the cockpit of a starfighter that flies forward on its own. Steer the crosshair with the arrow keys and fire at whatever comes toward you: enemy fighters, large asteroids that take four hits and small rocks that break with one.

Every collision and every enemy shot costs shield energy. Enemy fighters fire green plasma balls, which you can shoot down as well. Laser fences block half of the screen, and a hint on screen tells you which way to go.

Clear a sector to earn a bonus for the sector number and for the shield you have left. Your shield then recharges a little and you get one more bomb. Each new sector is longer and faster than the last.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Save your bombs for crowded moments: one bomb destroys every rock, fighter and plasma ball on screen, and you can carry up to three.
  2. Watch for the direction hint as soon as a laser fence appears. Steering early is much safer than steering late.
  3. Shoot the plasma balls. They are worth only 10 points, but every one you stop saves shield energy, and the shield pays at the end of the sector.
