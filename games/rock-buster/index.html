Rock Buster: a free Asteroids-style space shooter

Pilot a tiny ship through a field of drifting rocks. Every hit breaks a rock into smaller, faster pieces, flying saucers take shots at you, and a quick warp gets you out of trouble.

Play Rock Buster now How to play

How to play Rock Buster

Your ship starts in the middle of the screen, surrounded by large rocks. Turn left and right, fire your engine to move and shoot in the direction your nose points. Everything wraps around the edges: fly out on the left and you come back on the right.

A large rock breaks into two medium rocks, a medium one into two small ones, and small rocks vanish. The smaller the rock, the faster it moves and the more points it brings. Clear the screen and the next, bigger wave drifts in.

Flying saucers appear now and then and fire at you. The big one shoots at random, the small one aims. When there is no way out, press Shift to warp to a random spot on the screen.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Do not break every big rock at once. A screen full of small, fast rocks is much harder to survive than a few slow big ones.
  2. Use short bursts of thrust. Your ship keeps drifting, and a calm ship near the centre is easier to control.
  3. Warp is a gamble: you may land right next to a rock. Use it only when a hit is certain anyway.
