Paddle Duel: free Pong against the computer

The game that started it all: two paddles, one ball. Play table tennis against four computer opponents, from Rookie to Champion. First to seven points wins.

Play Paddle Duel now How to play

How to play Paddle Duel

You control the paddle on the left with the Up and Down keys. The computer plays on the right. Hit the ball back and get it past your opponent's paddle to score a point.

The first player to reach seven points wins the match. Every match you win brings a tougher opponent: Rookie, Challenger, Pro and finally the Champion, each faster and more accurate than the last.

Every return earns a few points, and long rallies raise the value of the point that ends them. Points and match wins are worth more against stronger opponents.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to send it at a steep angle. Straight returns are easy for the computer.
  2. Move back toward the middle after every return. From there you reach both corners in time.
  3. Patience pays: every hit in a rally adds to the value of the point that ends it.
