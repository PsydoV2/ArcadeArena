City Defender: a free Missile Command-style game

Missiles rain down on six cities. Move the crosshair, fire from your three bases and catch the attackers in the blast. Some missiles split on the way down.

Play City Defender now How to play

How to play City Defender

Six cities stand on the ground, protected by three missile bases. Enemy missiles fall from the sky toward them. Move the crosshair with the arrow keys and press Space: a missile flies from the nearest base and explodes where the crosshair was.

Any enemy missile that touches the cloud of an explosion is destroyed. Each base has a limited number of missiles per wave, so every shot should count. From wave three on, some enemy missiles split into several on the way down.

At the end of each wave, every missile you have left and every city still standing earns bonus points. The point multiplier rises every two waves, up to six times. The game ends when all six cities are gone.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Aim a little below a falling missile, not right at it. Your own missile needs time to get there, and the middle base fires the fastest ones.
  2. One explosion can catch several missiles. Wait until two trails cross, then fire at the crossing point.
  3. Catch splitting missiles high up, before they divide. After the split you need several shots instead of one.
