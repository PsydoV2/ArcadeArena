Gem Cascade: a free Columns-style puzzle game

Columns of three gems fall into the well. Swap their order, slide them left or right and line up three or more of a kind, across, down or diagonally.

Play Gem Cascade now How to play

How to play Gem Cascade

A column of three gems falls into a narrow well. Move it left or right and press Space to rotate the order of the three gems. Press Down to drop faster or Shift to drop it at once.

When three or more gems of the same kind line up, across, down or diagonally, they disappear. The gems above fall into the gap, and if that creates a new line, it clears too. These chains multiply your points.

Every gem color also has its own shape, so you can tell them apart even with color blindness. After every 30 cleared gems the level rises and the gems fall faster. The game ends when the well is full.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Keep the middle of the well low. A column that cannot get past a tall stack in the centre ends the game quickly.
  2. Look for diagonals. They are easy to miss and often clear gems that are stuck at the sides.
  3. Build chains on purpose: leave a pair of the same kind just above a line you are about to clear.
