Cosmic Siege: a free Space Invaders-style shooter

Waves of invaders march down the screen, faster with every row you clear. Hide behind the shields, watch for the mystery UFO and stop them before they land.

Play Cosmic Siege now How to play

How to play Cosmic Siege

You command a laser cannon at the bottom of the screen. Above you, five rows of aliens march sideways in formation. Each time the formation reaches the edge of the screen, it steps down one row closer to the ground.

Shoot them one by one. The fewer aliens are left, the faster the rest move, so the last few are always the hardest. Four orange shields protect you from enemy fire, but every hit, yours or theirs, chips a piece away.

From time to time a mystery UFO crosses the top of the screen. Hit it for a bonus between 50 and 300 points. If a single alien reaches the ground, the game is over, no matter how many lives you have left.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Clear the outer columns first. A narrower formation needs longer to reach the edge, so it steps down less often.
  2. Shoot through the bottom of a shield to make a firing slot, then fire from cover.
  3. The UFO's value depends on how many shots you have fired. Players who count their shots can collect the full 300 points.
  4. Do not waste shots on the last alien: it is very fast. Wait for it to come toward you and fire early.
