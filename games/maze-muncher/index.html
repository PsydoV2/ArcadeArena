Maze Muncher: a free Pac-Man-style maze game

Eat every dot in the maze. Four imps hunt you, each in its own way. Grab a power pill and the hunters become the hunted.

Play Maze Muncher now How to play

How to play Maze Muncher

Guide the muncher through a neon maze and eat every dot. When the maze is empty, the next level starts with faster imps. A tunnel on each side takes you across to the other side of the screen.

Four imps leave their house in the middle and chase you. Each has its own personality: some head straight for you, others try to cut you off. Every now and then they stop hunting for a few seconds and scatter to their corners.

The four large power pills in the corners turn the imps blue for a short time. Now you can eat them, and each one you catch in a row is worth twice as much. Twice per level a bonus fruit appears for a few seconds.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Do not eat a power pill as soon as you reach it. Wait until the imps are close, then eat it and turn around: all four in a row are worth 3,000 points.
  2. Clear the long corridors first and leave the dots near the power pills for last. That keeps an escape route open.
  3. Use the side tunnels. The imps slow down in them, you do not.
