Brick Blaster: a free Breakout-style brick breaker

Bounce the ball off your paddle and smash every brick on the wall. Hard bricks need two hits, steel never breaks, and falling capsules bring a wider paddle or extra balls.

Play Brick Blaster now How to play

How to play Brick Blaster

Move the paddle along the bottom of the screen and press Space to launch the ball. It bounces off the walls, the bricks and your paddle. Miss it and you lose a life.

Each level is a hand-built wall of bricks. Colored bricks break with one hit and pay more the higher their row. Light gray bricks need two hits, steel bricks never break. Clear every breakable brick to move on to the next level.

Some bricks drop a capsule when they break. Catch it with your paddle for a wider paddle, a multi ball, a slower ball or an extra life.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Where the ball hits the paddle decides its angle. Use the edges of the paddle to aim at the gaps.
  2. Break a hole through one side of the wall. Once the ball gets above the bricks, it clears them from behind on its own.
  3. Do not chase every capsule. Losing the ball to catch a power-up is never worth it.
