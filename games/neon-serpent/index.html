Neon Serpent: a free snake game in your browser

Steer a growing neon serpent around the screen. Every apple makes you longer and faster, walls change from level to level, and bonus stars are worth more the quicker you grab them.

Play Neon Serpent now How to play

How to play Neon Serpent

The serpent moves on its own. You only change its direction with the arrow keys. Steer it to the apple: it grows by one segment and speeds up a little. Hitting a wall or your own body costs a life.

Eat ten apples to clear a level. Each level has its own wall layout, five in total, and every cleared level pays a bonus. After the fifth layout the walls repeat, but the serpent keeps getting faster.

Now and then a bonus star appears. It is worth up to 2,000 points, but its value drops every second, so be quick.

Tips for a higher score

  1. Stay near the edges and walls instead of crossing the middle. That keeps the open space free for your growing tail.
  2. Plan two turns ahead. Most crashes happen when the serpent turns into a corridor it cannot leave.
  3. Do not chase every star. A star far away is worth little by the time you get there, and the detour can trap you.
