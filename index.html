Questions and answers

Is Arcade Arena free?

Yes. All games are free to play in your browser. There are no ads, no in-game purchases and no account to create.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Open the arcade hall in a modern browser such as Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari, pick a cabinet and press Enter. The game starts in seconds.

How do I control the games?

Every cabinet uses the same layout: arrow keys or W, A, S, D to move, Space as the first button and Shift as the second. Enter starts and pauses a game, Esc takes you back to the hall.

Can I play with a gamepad?

Yes. Xbox, PlayStation and Switch Pro controllers work. Use the D-pad or left stick to move, A to fire, B as the second button, Start to pause and Select to go back.

Are my high scores saved?

Yes, on your own computer. Each cabinet keeps a top 10 table with three-letter initials, stored in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Not yet. Arcade Arena is made for a desktop or laptop computer with a keyboard or gamepad. Touch controls are planned.

Are these the original arcade games?

No. Cosmic Siege, Maze Muncher and Star Run are original games inspired by golden-age classics such as Space Invaders and Pac-Man. The graphics, sounds and names are our own.

How long does a game take?

Most rounds last between 2 and 10 minutes. There is nothing to save or unlock, so you can stop any time and come back next week without starting to learn again.
